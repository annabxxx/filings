COLD CASE ROOM 001 • FEILDING • 2010

UNSOLVED • ACQUITTAL MUST BE RESPECTED

Who killed Scott Guy?

An unsolved killing deserves careful questions, not a replacement verdict assembled on the internet.

HEAR SAY case desk • Updated 24 August 2026

LEGAL STATUS

Ewen Macdonald was acquitted of Scott Guy’s murder by a jury on 3 July 2012. HEAR SAY does not treat an acquitted person as guilty.

The short chronology

8 JUL 2010

Scott Guy, 31, was killed at the end of his rural driveway near Feilding.

3 JUL 2012

A jury found Ewen Macdonald not guilty of murder. Police publicly said they accepted the verdict.

AFTER TRIAL

Police said that if anything new came to light it would be investigated, but at that time they were not pursuing anyone else or other lines of inquiry.

What is established

  • Scott Guy was murdered.
  • Macdonald was tried and acquitted.
  • An acquittal is not an unresolved accusation waiting to be re-labelled.
  • The public record can still be examined for what it does and does not establish.

What remains unknown

The central unanswered question is who killed Scott Guy. Public discussion becomes useful only when it distinguishes source material from speculation and says what evidence would falsify a theory.

The HEAR SAY test for any theory

1. SOURCE

What public document, testimony or verified fact supports it?

2. INFERENCE

What are you inferring rather than directly proving?

3. FAILURE TEST

What evidence would make you abandon the theory?

4. LEGAL STATUS

Does the wording accurately state verdicts and acquittals?
