THE APPROVED QUESTION

Did admitting the visual-identification evidence produce a miscarriage of justice?

01

What is before the Court?

Whether the Court of Appeal was correct to conclude no miscarriage of justice arose from the trial Judge's decision to admit Guy Wallace's visual-identification evidence.

02

What is not automatically before the Court?

Every theory, every disputed feature of the case, and every public argument made over the years. The grant of leave is narrower than the public debate.

03

What changes only if the Court says so?

The legal consequences of the appeal. Until a court changes the position, the convictions remain in place.
