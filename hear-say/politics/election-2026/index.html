OFFICIAL TIMELINE

The dates that actually matter.

Campaign noise changes daily. The statutory timetable does not.

NOWREGULATED PERIOD

7 August — regulated period began

The Electoral Commission says the three-month regulated period for election advertising runs from 7 August to midnight on 6 November. Spending limits apply to candidates, registered parties and third-party promoters.

1 OCTPARLIAMENT

Dissolution of Parliament

The official timetable records 1 October as dissolution, followed by writ day on 4 October and candidate nominations closing at noon on 8 October.

VOTINGOCT–NOV

21 October overseas • 26 October advance • 7 November election day

Voting opens overseas on 21 October, advance voting starts on 26 October, and election-day voting places are scheduled to open from 9am to 7pm on 7 November.

QUESTIONS WORTH TRACKING

Ask questions that can survive election night.

COST

What does the promise cost?

Not the headline. The fiscal cost, funding source, timing and assumptions.

DELIVERY

When would a voter know it happened?

A promise without a measurable delivery point is difficult to hold to account.

REVERSAL

Did the position change?

A policy change is not automatically hypocrisy. Put the old and new positions side by side and explain why they differ.

REPLY

What does the party or candidate say?

Substantive responses should sit beside the criticism they answer, not disappear into a separate page.
