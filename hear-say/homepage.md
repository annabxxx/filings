# HEAR SAY

## Your experience matters. Your voice belongs in the record.

Family Court proceedings can be intensely personal and difficult to navigate. Too often, people leave with questions about whether they were heard, whether procedures were followed, or whether their experience will ever be understood outside their own case.

**Hear Say is a place for people to speak about what happened to them.**

We are building a public, evidence-conscious record of experiences with the Family Court and related systems in Aotearoa New Zealand.

### Tell your story

Share what happened in your own words. You can submit anonymously or identify yourself where you choose.

**We want:**
- what happened
- when it happened
- which part of the system was involved
- what documents or records support your account
- what response you received
- what you want other people to understand

### Evidence matters

Stories will be separated from independently verified material. We will not present an allegation as an established fact. Where documents can lawfully be published, they can help readers understand the underlying record.

### Protect people

Do not submit identifying information about children or vulnerable people unless it is necessary and lawful. Remove private addresses, phone numbers, passwords, medical details and other sensitive information that does not need to be public.

### Why this exists

The purpose is simple: **people should be able to see what others have experienced, identify recurring issues, preserve evidence, and have informed conversations about accountability and reform.**

This is not about attacking individuals. It is about making experiences visible and creating a record that can be examined carefully.
