MODERATION

Evidence before accusation

Strong disagreement is welcome. Threats, doxxing, harassment and unsupported serious allegations are not.

What can be published

  • Source-backed corrections and evidence leads.
  • Clearly labelled opinion and lived experience.
  • Good-faith disagreement and substantive right of reply.
  • Case theories that accurately state legal status and identify what could disprove them.

What will be held back

  • Threats or harassment.
  • Private addresses or identifying details about children.
  • Restricted or unlawfully disclosed court material.
  • Serious accusations stated as fact without adequate support.

Corrections

If a contribution is materially wrong, the correction should remain visible with the original record rather than silently disappearing.
