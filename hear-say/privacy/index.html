PRIVACY

Share only what the story needs

Public-interest reporting does not require publishing every private detail a contributor possesses.

Public contributions

Approved comments may publish a name or pseudonym and the submitted response. Optional contact details are for follow-up and should not be displayed publicly.

Private submissions

A private story submission is not automatically a public story. Material should be reviewed for relevance, accuracy, legal restrictions and unnecessary identifying information before publication.

Do not submit

  • Passwords or authentication secrets.
  • Children’s identifying information unless clearly lawful and essential.
  • Private home addresses.
  • Restricted court material.
  • Medical or intimate information that the story does not genuinely require.
