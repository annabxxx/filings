THE RECORD

Story is not the same thing as proof.

That distinction is a strength, not a weakness. HEAR SAY should make it easy for a reader to see what is first-person experience, what is documented, what is disputed and whether a response has been received.

1

Account

What the contributor says happened, in their own words.

2

Evidence

Documents, dates and independently checkable material that can lawfully be published.

3

Response

Right of reply, correction, disagreement and context.

4

Outcome

What changed, what did not, and what remains unresolved.

Credibility comes from showing the reader where certainty ends.
