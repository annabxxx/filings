THE RECORD

Stories will be separated from facts that can be verified.

A person's account will be identified as an account. Documents and independently verifiable material will be treated separately. That distinction is deliberate: credibility comes from transparency, not from shouting the loudest.

EVIDENCE

Build a record, not a rumour.

Every contribution can eventually be structured around who, what, when, where, document, response and outcome. Python can help us analyse that structured information for duplicate records, dates, timelines, recurring themes and connections — without pretending that statistical patterns prove misconduct.

That gives HEAR SAY the potential to become much more than a collection of stories.
