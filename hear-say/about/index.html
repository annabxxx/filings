ABOUT

A public square with the labels left on

HEAR SAY is an independent public-interest beta publication built around source visibility, clear status labels, right of reply and visible correction.

Fact

A factual proposition should be linked to a reliable source where practicable.

Lived experience

A contributor’s account is identified as an account unless independently established.

Opinion

Analysis, opinion and reader theories are labelled rather than presented as findings.

Reply

A substantive response belongs beside the criticism it answers.

What HEAR SAY is trying to do

Make difficult public-interest subjects readable without flattening the distinction between evidence, allegation, uncertainty and outcome. The publication should be useful enough that a reader comes back because something has changed, not because the site shouts the loudest.

What HEAR SAY is not

It is not a court, investigation agency or substitute for legal advice. Publication does not determine guilt, liability or credibility.
